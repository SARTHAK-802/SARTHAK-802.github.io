Greetings! I am Sarthak Maity, I got my M.Sc. (2025) in Chemistry (specialization: computaional chemistry) at [Indian Institute of Technology Indore (IIT Indore)](https://www.iiti.ac.in/), India, advised by [Prof. Biswarup Pathak](https://people.iiti.ac.in/~biswarup/). Prior to joining IIT Indore, I received my B.Sc. in Chemistry from [Midnapore College (Autonomous)](https://midnaporecollege.ac.in/) in 2023, West Bengal, India.

My research focus is on the application of artificial intelligence and machine learning in materials and catalyst discovery. I am deeply engaged in generative models, machine learning interatomic potentials, and data-driven approaches for predicting materials properties. I intend to integrate AI with physics-based simulations to develop reliable and efficient workflows for the design and understanding of functional materials, molecules, and interfaces.
