Greetings! I am Sarthak Maity, I got my M.Sc. (2025) in Chemistry (specialization: computaional chemistry) at [Indian Institute of Technology Indore (IIT Indore)](https://www.iiti.ac.in/), India, advised by [Prof. Biswarup Pathak](https://people.iiti.ac.in/~biswarup/). Prior to joining IIT Indore, I received my B.Sc. in Chemistry from [Midnapore College (Autonomous)](https://midnaporecollege.ac.in/) in 2023, West Bengal, India.

My research interests revolve around leveraging artificial intelligence to advance the discovery and design of functional materials and catalysts. I am especially interested in integrating machine learning models, such as generative approaches and ML interatomic potentials, with first-principles and physics-informed simulations. Through this, I aim to develop reliable, data-driven workflows for property prediction and materials screening, and to contribute to AI-driven closed-loop discovery systems that can autonomously guide the exploration of molecules, materials, and electrochemical interfaces.
